## Description

Give your practice a moment of its own with Meditation Timer. Choose how long you want to sit,
start the clock, and let a soft gong tell you when the time is over.

• Sessions from 1 to 180 minutes, with quick durations to get started.
• Opening and closing gong, each switched on separately.
• Looping background sound, chosen right under the countdown, fading gently in and out.
• Volume control and a preview, shared by the gong and the background sound.
• Pause, resume and finish, with your practice time saved without the pauses.
• A calendar of minutes per day, a date picker and the list of your sessions.
• Night and day themes, and four accent colours.
• Six languages: Spanish, Catalan/Valencian, English, Dutch, French and Russian.
• Automatic Do Not Disturb on Android, restored when you finish.
• Keeps the screen awake while you sit, if you want it to.

No ads, no accounts, no guided meditations and no tracking. Your preferences and your practice
stay on your device.
